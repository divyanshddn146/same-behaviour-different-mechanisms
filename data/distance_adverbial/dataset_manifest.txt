================================================================================
ADVERBIAL DISTANCE AGREEMENT DATASET
================================================================================

Purpose:
  Increase subject-to-verb distance without adding extra noun attractors.

Template:
  The {subject} of the {distractor}{adverbial_filler}

Target next-token auxiliaries:
  be_present: is / are
  be_past:    was / were

Distance fillers:
  short      | level=0 | filler='' | base template
  medium     | level=1 | filler=', quite clearly,' | short parenthetical adverbial
  long       | level=2 | filler=', quite clearly, rather obviously, as expected,' | longer parenthetical adverbial chain
  extra_long | level=3 | filler=', quite clearly, rather obviously, as expected, at least for now, in most cases,' | extra-long parenthetical adverbial chain

Important design choices:
  - No extra noun attractors in the filler.
  - No trailing space in prompt.
  - Score target as prompt + ' is', prompt + ' are', etc.
  - Behavioural audit required before causal analysis.
